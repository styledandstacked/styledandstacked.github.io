Good to know

Quick answers

Is it editable or fillable?

No. It is a print-and-write planner: print it and fill it in by hand. Nothing calculates automatically, so write totals yourself.

Do I need every page?

No. The Start Here page offers two routes, and every page works on its own. Print only what fits your shower.

Which file should I print?

Open the folder for your paper size (US Letter or A4), then choose Color or Low-Ink. Print at Actual Size (100%) and test-print one page first.

Does it include games?

No. This is a planner. The party games are a separate product, shown below.
